Reveal-and-rail paneling looks simple until you're the one holding the story pole, trying to keep every reveal the same width around outlets, corners, and a wall that isn't quite square. We cut the panels and rails to a fixed layout off your elevation, so the spacing is consistent from the first sheet to the last.

Send an elevation, wall dimensions, and your material and finish plan, and we'll turn it into a shop drawing that locks in the layout before anything is cut.
