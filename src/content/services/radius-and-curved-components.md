A radius opening is one of the only things on a job site that's genuinely hard to build with a circular saw and a story pole. Most crews still lay it out by hand, cut it with a jigsaw, and hope the curve reads clean once it's painted. It usually doesn't — and if the opening is symmetrical, the two sides rarely match.

We cut arch rafters, radius headers, curved casings, and curved trim on the CNC from a dimensioned drawing, so the curve is exact and repeatable. Send an architect's detail, a radius and springline, or even just a photo of the rough opening, and we'll turn it into a shop drawing you approve before anything gets cut. The parts that come back drop into place — no on-site scribing, no second curve to match.
