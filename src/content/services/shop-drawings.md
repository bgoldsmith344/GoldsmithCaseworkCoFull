An architect's drawing tells you what a part should look like when it's installed. It doesn't tell a machine how to cut it. That translation — from design intent to dimensioned, buildable geometry — is where the real work happens, and it's where mistakes get caught before they're expensive.

Every job starts here, whether it's an architect's set, a contractor's sketch, or a photo of a rough opening with a tape measure in frame. We draft a dimensioned shop drawing and send it back for your approval before anything is cut, so what shows up on site matches what everyone signed off on.
