Some parts don't belong in a production run — a router template for a detail you'll cut by hand, a fixture that holds a part square, a single odd-shaped piece that doesn't match anything in a catalog. Those are exactly the jobs a CNC is good for and a table saw isn't.

Send a sketch, a sample, or just rough dimensions and a description of how the part will be used, and we'll cut it — one piece or a short run, either way.
