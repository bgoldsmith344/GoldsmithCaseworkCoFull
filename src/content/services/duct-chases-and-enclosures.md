Boxing in a duct run or wrapping a beam almost always turns into an on-site build, because the geometry isn't fully known until the mechanicals are in. We work from your rough dimensions and site photos to draft the enclosure ahead of time, then cut it so it goes together on install day instead of being built around in place.

Send dimensions and a few photos of the space, and we'll draft a shop drawing that accounts for the real geometry — not just the plan-set version of it.
